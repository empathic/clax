This Privacy Policy describes how Empathic (“we,” “us,” “our,” or “Empathic”) handles personal information that we collect through the hosted Clax service at [https://clax.dev](https://clax.dev), the Clax project at [https://github.com/empathic/clax](https://github.com/empathic/clax), and any other sites or services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Services”).

**CLAX ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER**

When you run Clax yourself, on your own computer or on a server you operate, it is not one of our Services, and we receive none of the information it stores or handles. Clax keeps your pages, versions, comments, page data and settings in a directory on that computer (by default `~/.clax`). It contains no analytics or telemetry and does not report your use to us. It connects to other services only when you or your agents set it up to:

- **Downloading Clax.** The Clax plugins for your coding agents, and the installer, download the Clax program from GitHub’s release servers. Installing the Pi package from npm, or Clax from crates.io, contacts those registries. GitHub, npm and crates.io receive the request, including your IP address, under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it.
- **Pages that ask an AI model.** A page that uses Clax’s `sample` capability sends the request, with the API key you configure, to the model provider you choose (Anthropic by default). That provider’s privacy policy applies.
- **Publishing to another Clax.** If you connect your Clax to a hosted Clax, such as ours at clax.dev, what you publish there goes to that server. If that server is ours, this Privacy Policy covers it.
- **People on your network.** If you serve Clax on your network and invite other people, they see what you share with them, and their names and comments are stored on your computer. If you connect your Clax to someone else’s (`clax connect`), what you and your agents send there is stored on their computer.
- **Git remotes.** If you clone an artifact’s versions or push them with git, they go to the remote you choose.

The rest of this Privacy Policy describes the information we collect through our Services.
